# ResoFit OS — Tier 1 → Tier X Member Journey

## Product philosophy

ResoFit is a personalized wellness platform built around the Coach Buchi / Resonance philosophy:

> **Resonance before intensity. Consistency before perfection. The person comes before the program.**

The system should meet a member where they are, understand their lifestyle, reduce friction, create small wins, and progressively increase responsibility only when the member is ready.

The goal is not to overwhelm a new member with products. The goal is to create a measurable relationship:

**Welcome → Understand → Personalize → Act → Reflect → Adapt → Progress → Share → Ascend → Lead.**

The ₦1,000 entry experience is the activation point. It must deliver immediate value and create a seven-day proof period before commercial upsell.

---

## Core member principles

1. **Person first:** goals, age, ability, schedule, food culture, access, confidence and constraints shape the experience.
2. **Nigeria-first personalization:** recommendations should work with realistic Nigerian foods, homes, gyms, budgets and routines.
3. **Resonance:** the system adapts to the member rather than forcing the member into a rigid template.
4. **Progressive overload without reckless escalation:** physical progression must respect readiness and accessibility.
5. **One clear next action:** every screen should answer “What should I do now?”
6. **Accountability without shame:** missed days trigger support and adjustment, never punishment.
7. **Evidence before upsell:** commercial upgrades appear after demonstrated value, with the default first upsell beginning on Day 7.
8. **Privacy and consent:** health, biometric, lifestyle and social information must never be exposed or shared without explicit consent.
9. **Professional escalation:** medical concerns are routed to qualified professionals; CoachB2K does not diagnose or replace clinicians.
10. **Community creates momentum:** social sharing is optional, consent-based and rewarded as a milestone rather than required for care.

# XP architecture

XP is a behavioural progress layer, not a measure of medical health or human worth.

Recommended event model:

```text
member_id
milestone_id
xp_awarded
source
occurred_at
idempotency_key
metadata
```

### Required properties
- Every milestone is idempotent.
- XP cannot be duplicated by refresh/retry.
- Server-side persistence is preferred over localStorage for production accounts.
- XP changes are auditable.
- Tier transitions are deterministic.
- Users can see why XP was awarded.
- Social sharing XP requires a completed share action and user consent.

# Dashboard requirements

The dashboard should always make five things obvious:

1. **Who am I?**
2. **Where am I in my journey?**
3. **What have I achieved?**
4. **What should I do today?**
5. **What is my next meaningful milestone?**

Primary dashboard hierarchy:

**Today → XP → Current Tier → Today's Actions → Progress → CoachB2K → Plan → Community → Upgrade (only when eligible).**

# Accessibility & personalization

Every tier should respect:
- reduced motion,
- larger text,
- high contrast,
- screen readers,
- keyboard navigation,
- touch target sizing,
- light/dark mode,
- device capabilities,
- age-appropriate presentation,
- gender/presentation preferences without stereotyping,
- physical limitations and declared accessibility needs.

Never infer sensitive health characteristics merely from appearance, device data or demographics.
